# Multi frame recognition failure curve pilot

## Purpose and evidence limits

This benchmark separates vehicle tracking, plate localization, OCR candidate
ranking and final acceptance failures. It evaluates multi-frame recognition only.
It never uploads events or saves Sentinel media. The manually annotated crop
stage is a diagnostic, not an operator feature or product accuracy result.

The initial development input is three distinct video-derived images of one
Indian vehicle. NIVU publishes the source dataset under CC BY 4.0:
https://universe.roboflow.com/nivu/indian-license-plate-knte7 . Attribution:
NIVU, Indian License Plate Dataset, Roboflow Universe, April 2022.
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/ . Cropping and controlled
degradation are modifications for this benchmark. Source media, labels and local
reports stay under ignored `data/benchmarks`; they are not distributed in Git.

These exports are vehicle-centred crops, not full traffic video. Original FPS
and presentation timestamps are unknown. The manifest explicitly uses
`assigned_replay` with 400 ms spacing. This permits testing evidence fusion and
spatial degradation, but proves neither live visibility duration nor alert
latency. Three different frames are not three independent vehicles, and all
degraded variants remain the same development passage. There is no held-out
accuracy estimate. Labels and approximate boxes were manually inspected, not
generated by the model. No label is passed to neural inference.

## Method

1. Require explicit provenance, permission, Indian labels and a bounded passage.
   Reject retained Sentinel inputs, path traversal, duplicate stills and unordered
   timestamps. Keep a vehicle and its plate in one development/test split.
2. Check annotations against decoded dimensions. Pad varying export dimensions
   onto one fixed canvas without stretching the original content. Always select
   ByteTrack explicitly, including during warm-up.
3. Sample once using declared timestamps. Reset tracker, fusion, metrics and
   deduplication state between conditions. Bound decoded passage memory to
   384 MiB and 1,800 frames; use one GPU experiment at a time.
4. Degrade full source frames deterministically, before detection. Evaluate
   resolution scales 1, .75, .5, .375, .25, .1875 and .125; Gaussian kernels
   3/5/7/9; horizontal motion kernels 3/5/9/15; noise sigma 5/15/30; brightness
   multipliers .75/.5/.25. These are engineering controls, not universal camera
   quality ratings. Compression is not implemented.
5. Run the end-to-end pipeline and independently the `oracle_plate_ocr` stage.
   The latter reads human-marked plate crops and bypasses localization. Compare
   its failures with end-to-end failures; do not report it as automatic detection.
6. Associate outputs to the labelled vehicle with vehicle-box IoU >= .5.
   Target-specific evidence determines failure classification; all-scene counters
   remain separate. Approximate boxes and this matching rule are pilot limitations.
7. Preserve production acceptance gates: at least three separated observations,
   mean character score >= .85, weakest character >= .75, repeated character
   support >= .60 in two observations, and candidate log margin >= .5.
   Unsupported lengths/formats, ranking ambiguity, absent candidates, insufficient
   evidence and weak acceptance are reported separately.
8. Score exact whole-plate correctness, wrong emissions and abstentions separately.
   Final-window candidate inclusion is not passage-level recall at 200. Reports
   contain numeric results, model versions, thresholds and timing provenance,
   but no images, target text, predicted text, source URLs or input paths.

## Production fixes from the critical fixes review

Temporal v2 applies registration grammar while constructing the bounded beam,
so invalid prefixes cannot crowd out valid candidates. Filtering never
renormalizes scores. Trailing end/padding slots must have repeatable evidence;
an uncertain plate length cannot hide behind a confident prefix.

Before fusion, abrupt vehicle class, area, aspect-ratio or position changes clear
the reader. A gap greater than two seconds of video time also clears it. These
heuristic discontinuity checks reduce mixed-track risk, but do not prove identity
and can discard useful evidence during real camera motion. They do not reset
the forty-attempt budget. The existing per-plate spatial check remains.

After forty unsuccessful attempts, a track gets the internal reason
`attempt_budget_exhausted` and increments `no_read_tracks` once. This is numeric
diagnostic state, not a suspicious-vehicle alert or new dashboard event. Track
expiry and active-state caps remain. Frames, crops and unmasked text stay in RAM.

Unit tests demonstrate grammar, padding, motion-reset, time-gap and bounded-retry
behaviour using synthetic probabilities and mocks. They do not demonstrate a
gain in real plate accuracy. No threshold was lowered.

## PARSeq comparison

PARSeq is an opt-in research adapter, not the live worker's default or an
automatically downloaded production dependency. Official source revision:
`1902db043c029a7e03a3818c616c06600af574be` from https://github.com/baudm/parseq .
The author's `parseq-bb5792a6.pt` weights are cached by the official loader with
its hash check. The model is evaluated on CUDA with a constant twelve-character
capacity plus an end token; capacity is never taken from a target label.

The adapter uses official bicubic resize, RGB conversion, tensor conversion and
0.5 normalization. Upper/lowercase probability mass is merged because plate
comparison is case-insensitive. Unsupported characters retain their mass in an
unknown token, which grammar cannot accept. The first predicted end-token score
is preserved; subsequent slots are non-text padding. The same temporal evidence
and acceptance gates are applied. This is per-frame deep OCR plus temporal
probability fusion, not a trained multi-frame restoration network.

Research dependencies are isolated under `/models/experiments/deps`: timm
0.9.16, pytorch-lightning 2.2.0.post0, torchmetrics 1.3.1, lightning-utilities
0.10.1, nltk 3.8.1, and their small supporting packages. Production Torch/CUDA
were not replaced. On this Torch build, initial model setup failed because a
native-JIT operator required a C compiler. The installed Torch source documents
`TORCH_DISABLE_NATIVE_JIT=1`; setting it for the experiment allowed evaluation
without installing a compiler or changing production settings.

## Reproduction

Supply a local licensed manifest following `data/benchmarks/nivu/manifest.json`;
the private pilot manifest/media are deliberately absent from Git. A sequence
requires timing_mode, ordered frames, manually verified ground_truth and
vehicle_box annotations. The oracle stage also needs plate_box annotations.
Do not relabel repeated stills as video or guess verified PTS.

After building the live image, use a one-off edge-worker container with the
dataset mounted read-only at `/bench` and a writable report directory at
`/reports`:

```text
python -m sentinel_edge benchmark-degradation --manifest /bench/manifest.json \
  --degradation-family resolution --report /reports/resolution.json
```

Repeat with blur, motion, noise and darkness. `--ocr-backend parseq` selects the
isolated experiment; its container additionally requires
`PYTHONPATH=/models/experiments/deps:/app` and `TORCH_DISABLE_NATIVE_JIT=1`.
The default remains CCT. Do not silently compare experiments that use different
thresholds, frames or pipeline revisions. Temporarily stop live GPU inference
and restore it in a `finally` block; do not stop the database or relays.

## Next evidence needed

A credible accuracy study needs independently labelled, permitted original
vehicle passages, verified video timestamps and more vehicles/cameras than this
pilot. Separate development and held-out test vehicles before choosing models or
thresholds. Include clear, blurred, night, angled and double-row plates. Measure
whole-plate accepted precision and coverage as well as character errors and
candidate recall. Fit confidence calibration only on development data and
evaluate it on untouched test data. A registry cannot recover a correct plate
that was never retained among the candidates.
